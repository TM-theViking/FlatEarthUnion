# FlatEarthUnion (FEU) - Document Archive

## 🏛️ Mission
The FlatEarthUnion is the idea, step one is a decentralized archive dedicated to the preservation of documents authored by flatEarthers. 
Not everyone has the time to do the research themselves, at least this way they have a good foundation to start from.

Our goal is not "debate", but the **documentation & archival of facts & logical arguments** for setting the foundation for future projects.

## 📁 Archive Structure
Documents are organised into the following broad categories:
*   **Celestial-Navigation** ('FEU-Documents/Celestial-Navigation/')
*   **Geography** ('FEU-Documents/Geography/')
*   **NaturalScience-ScientificMethod** ('FEU-Documents/NaturalScience-ScientificMethod/')
*   **Physics** ('FEU-Documents/Physics/')

## 📥 How to Contribute
1.  **Fork** this repository.
2.  Add your document to the appropriate category folder.
3.  Submit a **Pull Request**.
4.  Our moderators will review and merge the document into the main archive.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
This repository is an **archival project**. The documents contained herein are preserved for historical and research purposes.
